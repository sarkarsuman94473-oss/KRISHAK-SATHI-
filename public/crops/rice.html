Rice (Paddy) Cultivation & Protection Guide

Complete cultivation stages, nursery raising, SRI spacing, blast & BPH pest identification, and ICAR/BCKV verified IPM care for Indian rice farmers.

Last Reviewed & Updated: 2026-11-18

Agronomic Profile

Season: Kharif (June–Nov) & Boro (Dec–April)

Soil: Clay loam & alluvial soil, pH 5.5 – 6.8 with bunded water retention

Climate: 22°C – 32°C, high humidity during vegetative and panicle stages

Harvest Cycle: 110 – 145 days depending on variety (Swarna, Minikit, Gobindobhog)

Organic & Preventive Practices

Incorporate Azolla, Trichoderma-enriched compost, neem cake in basal dressing, and perch sticks (T-bars) for insectivorous birds.

Cultivation Guide

  1. Select certified treated seeds (e.g., Swarna Sub-1, Shatabdi/IET 4786, Gobindobhog) and conduct salt-water flotation test to discard unfilled grains.
  2. Raise healthy mat or wet nursery beds and transplant 21–25 day seedlings at 20 cm × 15 cm spacing (25 cm × 25 cm for SRI).
  3. Adopt alternate wetting and drying (AWD) during vegetative phase; maintain 2–5 cm water at panicle initiation and flowering.
  4. Apply split nitrogen doses based on Soil Health Card and Leaf Color Chart (LCC) to avoid lush foliage that attracts pests.

Major Pests & Diseases

  • Brown Planthopper (BPH): Causes 'hopper burn' circular drying patches; maintain alleyways and avoid excess nitrogen.
  • Yellow Stem Borer: Produces 'dead heart' at vegetative stage and 'white ear' at panicle stage; install pheromone traps.
  • Rice Blast & Sheath Blight: Spindle-shaped leaf spots or water-soaked sheath lesions; ensure proper drainage and resistant varieties.
Safety Notice: Always verify pesticide registration, active ingredients, and exact per-hectare label dosages on official CIBRC (cibrc.gov.in) product labels or consult your nearest BCKV / UBKV / KVK agronomist before spraying. Never exceed recommended Waiting Periods (PHI).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal seedling age for transplanting Kharif paddy?

For conventional transplanting, 21–25 day old seedlings give strong tillering; for System of Rice Intensification (SRI), transplant young 10–14 day seedlings.

How can farmers detect Brown Planthopper (BPH) early?

Part rice hills near the water line once a week in the morning to inspect stem bases for tiny brown hoppers before yellowing or hopper burn spreads.

Which nutrient deficiency turns lower older rice leaves yellow uniformly?

Uniform yellowing starting from older lower leaves usually indicates Nitrogen deficiency; always confirm with Soil Health Card or Leaf Color Chart before top-dressing.
