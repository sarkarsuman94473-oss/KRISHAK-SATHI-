Safe Pesticide Handling & Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

Evidence-based IPM hierarchy, CIBRC label verification rules, Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI), and PPE safety for Indian farmers.

Last Reviewed & Updated: 2026-11-18

Key Best Practices

  • Follow IPM hierarchy first: resistant varieties, clean seed, pheromone/sticky traps, border crops, and beneficial insects before chemical sprays.
  • Always check the CIBRC (Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee) color triangle and exact crop-pest match on the bottle label.
  • Never guess or extrapolate pesticide dosages across different crops — confirm exact dilution and Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) before harvesting produce.
  • Wear gloves, mask, long sleeves, and spray perpendicular to mild wind during early morning or late afternoon.
Safety Notice: Always verify pesticide registration, active ingredients, and exact per-hectare label dosages on official CIBRC (cibrc.gov.in) product labels or consult your nearest BCKV / UBKV / KVK agronomist before spraying. Never exceed recommended Waiting Periods (PHI).
