Solanum lycopersicum

Tomato Cultivation, Staking & Disease Prevention

High-yield tomato raised-bed nursery, mulching, Blossom End Rot nutrition, Whitefly Leaf Curl Virus & Fruit Borer trap crop IPM guide.

Last Reviewed & Updated: 2026-11-18

Agronomic Profile

Season: Autumn–Winter (Sept–Feb) & Monsoon–Spring hybrids

Soil: Fertile sandy loam to loam (pH 6.0 – 7.0), well-drained beds

Climate: 20°C – 28°C optimum fruit set temperature

Harvest Cycle: 65 – 90 days after transplanting

Organic & Preventive Practices

Marigold border trap crops, insect-proof seedling nets, Trichoderma soil drenching, and pheromone traps for Helicoverpa monitoring.

Cultivation Guide

  1. Raise seedlings under insect-proof 50-mesh net nursery to block Whitefly early in the crop cycle.
  2. Transplant 25-day seedlings onto raised beds with black silver mulch at 60 cm × 45 cm spacing.
  3. Install bamboo staking and twine tying for indeterminate hybrids to keep fruits clean off wet soil.
  4. Maintain uniform irrigation and calcium nutrition to prevent Blossom End Rot (dark sunken bottom on fruits).

Major Pests & Diseases

  • Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV): Transmitted by Whitefly; roguing infected plants early and yellow sticky traps are vital.
  • Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa): Larvae bore into green & ripe fruits; plant 1 row of African marigold as a trap crop per 15 rows.
  • Early Blight & Bacterial Wilt: Concentric leaf rings or sudden wilting without yellowing.
Safety Notice: Always verify pesticide registration, active ingredients, and exact per-hectare label dosages on official CIBRC (cibrc.gov.in) product labels or consult your nearest BCKV / UBKV / KVK agronomist before spraying. Never exceed recommended Waiting Periods (PHI).

Frequently Asked Questions

What causes black sunken rot at the bottom of tomato fruits?

Blossom End Rot is triggered by calcium deficiency and irregular watering rather than a fungal infection.

Why grow marigold flowers alongside tomato beds?

African marigold attracts female Fruit Borer moths to lay eggs on flowers instead of tomato clusters.

Can viral leaf curl be cured by fungicide?

No, Leaf Curl is a viral disease spread by Whitefly; focus on netted nurseries, roguing early cases, and vector control.
