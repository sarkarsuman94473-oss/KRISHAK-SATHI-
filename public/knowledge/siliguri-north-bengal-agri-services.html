Siliguri & North Bengal Agricultural Services & Nursery Ecosystem

Regional farming support across Siliguri, Jalpaiguri, Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Cooch Behar & Alipurduar: tea, pineapple, potato, seeds, and nurseries.

Last Reviewed & Updated: 2026-11-18

Key Best Practices

  • Covers key North Bengal agro-climatic Terai, Dooars, and Hill belts around Siliguri, Matigara, Naxalbari, Jalpaiguri, and Malbazar.
  • Connects growers with nursery seedlings, vegetable/flower saplings, soil & pest guidance, and regional wholesale mandi price tracking.
  • Supported scientifically by institutions like Uttar Banga Krishi Viswavidyalaya (UBKV) Pundibari, Tea Research Association (TRA), and district KVKs.
Safety Notice: Always verify pesticide registration, active ingredients, and exact per-hectare label dosages on official CIBRC (cibrc.gov.in) product labels or consult your nearest BCKV / UBKV / KVK agronomist before spraying. Never exceed recommended Waiting Periods (PHI).
