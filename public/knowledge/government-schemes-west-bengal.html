Central & West Bengal Government Farmer Schemes Guide

Official portal overview for PM-KISAN, Krishak Bandhu (West Bengal), Bangla Shasya Bima (BSB), PMFBY, KCC, and Soil Health Card.

Last Reviewed & Updated: 2026-11-18

Key Best Practices

  • PM-KISAN (pmkisan.gov.in): Income support to eligible landholding farmer families across India with e-KYC verification.
  • Krishak Bandhu (krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in): West Bengal assured financial support in Rabi & Kharif plus farmer death benefit cover.
  • Bangla Shasya Bima (banglarshasyabima.net): State crop insurance protecting registered farmers against natural calamities.
  • Always verify application deadlines and documents directly at official .gov.in / .wb.gov.in portals or your Block Agriculture Office.
Safety Notice: Always verify pesticide registration, active ingredients, and exact per-hectare label dosages on official CIBRC (cibrc.gov.in) product labels or consult your nearest BCKV / UBKV / KVK agronomist before spraying. Never exceed recommended Waiting Periods (PHI).
