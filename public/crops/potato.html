Solanum tuberosum

Potato (Aloo) Cultivation & Late Blight Care

West Bengal & North Bengal Rabi potato planting guide: seed tuber treatment, ridge earthing-up, frost/late blight scouting, and storage safety.

Last Reviewed & Updated: 2026-11-18

Agronomic Profile

Season: Rabi (Late October – February/March)

Soil: Well-drained sandy loam to silt loam, pH 5.5 – 6.5

Climate: Cool tuberization weather 15°C – 22°C

Harvest Cycle: 85 – 110 days (Kufri Jyoti, Kufri Chandramukhi, Kufri Pukhraj)

Organic & Preventive Practices

Follow 2–3 year crop rotation with rice or pulses, apply Trichoderma viride with well-rotted FYM, and clear volunteer haulms before storage.

Cultivation Guide

  1. Use disease-free certified seed tubers (35–45g with 2–3 strong sprouts); avoid cutting tubers if soil moisture is very high.
  2. Plant on ridges at 60 cm row-to-row and 20 cm plant-to-plant spacing for proper tuber expansion.
  3. Complete earthing-up at 25–30 days after planting so developing tubers remain covered and do not turn green.
  4. Give light furrow irrigation at stolon formation, tuber initiation, and bulking; stop irrigation 10–12 days before harvest.

Major Pests & Diseases

  • Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans): Dark water-soaked lesions with white fungal ring underneath leaves during foggy cold spells.
  • Cutworm & Aphids: Cut stems at ground level or transmit viral leaf roll; use yellow sticky traps and evening scouting.
  • Common Scab & Black Scurf: Prevent by rotating potato with paddy/maize and using clean seed.
Safety Notice: Always verify pesticide registration, active ingredients, and exact per-hectare label dosages on official CIBRC (cibrc.gov.in) product labels or consult your nearest BCKV / UBKV / KVK agronomist before spraying. Never exceed recommended Waiting Periods (PHI).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do potato tubers turn green and bitter?

Exposed tubers produce chlorophyll and toxic solanine when ridges erode; timely earthing-up 25–30 days after planting prevents greening.

When is Late Blight risk highest in West Bengal?

Continuous fog, cloudy skies, high humidity (>85%), and night temperatures between 10°C–16°C trigger rapid Late Blight spread.

Why should irrigation stop 10 days before potato harvest?

Stopping irrigation cures the tuber skin so potatoes resist bruising and storage rot.
