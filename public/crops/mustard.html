Brassica juncea

Indian Mustard (Toria / Sarson) Cultivation Guide

Low-water Rabi oilseed cultivation: timely sowing to beat Mustard Aphid, Sulphur nutrition for higher oil % and Alternaria blight protection.

Last Reviewed & Updated: 2026-11-18

Agronomic Profile

Season: Rabi (Mid-October – November sowing)

Soil: Medium sandy loam to loam (pH 6.0 – 7.5)

Climate: Cool clear weather during flowering & siliqua fill (15°C – 25°C)

Harvest Cycle: 95 – 120 days (B-9 / Benoy, Agrani, PM-28)

Organic & Preventive Practices

Early timely sowing, yellow sticky traps, clipping early aphid colonies manually, and neem sprays in late afternoon to protect honeybees.

Cultivation Guide

  1. Sow early (mid-October to first week of November) in rows 30 cm apart to escape peak winter Mustard Aphid infestation.
  2. Thin extra seedlings at 15–20 days to maintain 10–12 cm plant spacing.
  3. Apply Sulphur alongside NPK fertilizers to significantly boost oil content in seeds.
  4. Avoid spraying hazardous insecticides during peak bee pollination hours (10 AM – 3 PM).

Major Pests & Diseases

  • Mustard Aphid (Lipaphis erysimi): Dense colonies sucking flower stalks in cool cloudy weather; clip early infested twigs.
  • Alternaria Blight & White Rust: Concentric dark spots on leaves and pods.
Safety Notice: Always verify pesticide registration, active ingredients, and exact per-hectare label dosages on official CIBRC (cibrc.gov.in) product labels or consult your nearest BCKV / UBKV / KVK agronomist before spraying. Never exceed recommended Waiting Periods (PHI).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Sulphur important for mustard oilseed crops?

Sulphur directly supports oil biosynthesis and glucosinolate balance, improving seed oil percentage.

How does October sowing reduce Mustard Aphid damage?

Early-sown crops cross the tender flowering stage before peak February aphid outbreaks.

When should mustard crop be harvested to prevent shattering?

Harvest when 75–80% of siliquae (pods) turn golden yellow, preferably in the early morning.
