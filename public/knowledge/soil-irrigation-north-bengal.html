Soil Health, Acidic Soil Management & Irrigation Scheduling

Manage Terai/North Bengal acidic soils, Soil Health Card NPK+micronutrient balance, raised-bed drainage, and critical crop irrigation stages.

Last Reviewed & Updated: 2026-11-18

Key Best Practices

  • Test soil every 2–3 years under Soil Health Card; sub-Himalayan Terai/Dooars soils are frequently acidic (pH 4.8–5.8).
  • Apply agricultural lime (dolomite) based on lab soil test recommendations for vegetable & pulse plots (avoid lime in Tea gardens which prefer acidic pH).
  • Schedule irrigation around critical stages: crown root initiation in wheat, tillering/panicle in paddy, and stolon/bulking in potato.
Safety Notice: Always verify pesticide registration, active ingredients, and exact per-hectare label dosages on official CIBRC (cibrc.gov.in) product labels or consult your nearest BCKV / UBKV / KVK agronomist before spraying. Never exceed recommended Waiting Periods (PHI).
