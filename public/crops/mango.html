Mangifera indica

Mango Orchard Management & Pre-Harvest Care

Malda & Bengal mango guide: canopy pruning, Mango Hopper control, pheromone traps for Fruit Fly, Powdery Mildew, and Black Tip prevention.

Last Reviewed & Updated: 2026-11-18

Agronomic Profile

Season: Flowering: Jan–March; Harvest: May–July (Himsagar, Langra, Amrapali)

Soil: Deep fertile alluvial / loamy soil (pH 5.5 – 7.5), zero standing water

Climate: Dry winter & warm spring-summer (24°C – 35°C)

Harvest Cycle: 90 – 110 days after fruit set

Organic & Preventive Practices

Clean fallen fruits daily, hang methyl eugenol traps (10 per hectare), wrap sticky tree bands against Mealybug crawlers.

Cultivation Guide

  1. Prune overcrowded central branches after harvest so sunlight enters the orchard canopy.
  2. Plough orchard floor gently during winter to expose pupating Mango Mealybug and Fruit Fly stages.
  3. Irrigate sparingly only after pea-size fruit set to minimize premature fruit drop.
  4. Keep orchards clear of brick kiln smoke or provide borax nutrition if Black Tip symptoms occur.

Major Pests & Diseases

  • Mango Hopper & Powdery Mildew: Causes honeydew sooty mold and panicle blight during flowering.
  • Mango Fruit Fly (Bactrocera dorsalis): Maggots spoil ripening fruit; hang methyl eugenol pheromone traps before color break.
  • Anthracnose: Black sunken spots on leaves and ripening skins.
Safety Notice: Always verify pesticide registration, active ingredients, and exact per-hectare label dosages on official CIBRC (cibrc.gov.in) product labels or consult your nearest BCKV / UBKV / KVK agronomist before spraying. Never exceed recommended Waiting Periods (PHI).

Frequently Asked Questions

How do pheromone traps stop Mango Fruit Fly?

Methyl eugenol traps lure male fruit flies early in fruit maturity, cutting down egg-laying inside ripe mangoes.

When should post-harvest mango canopy pruning happen?

Prune deadwood and crossing interior twigs right after harvest before fresh monsoon flushes.

Why should sprays be avoided during peak mango full-bloom pollination?

Bees and flies pollinate mango panicles; spraying during active bloom reduces fruit set.
