Capsicum annuum

Chilli (Lanka / Mirch) Cultivation & Murda Complex Guide

Solve chilli upward/downward leaf curling by distinguishing Thrips vs Yellow Mite, plus nursery hygiene, drainage & Anthracnose dieback control.

Last Reviewed & Updated: 2026-11-18

Agronomic Profile

Season: Kharif (June–Oct) & Rabi/Pre-Summer (Sept–March)

Soil: Well-drained sandy loam to black loam (pH 6.0 – 7.0)

Climate: 20°C – 30°C warm weather without water stagnation

Harvest Cycle: 65 – 85 days first green picking

Organic & Preventive Practices

Maize border barrier rows, blue + yellow sticky traps, neem oil sprays, and removing early virus-infected bushes.

Cultivation Guide

  1. Transplant 30–35 day old seedlings onto raised beds with strict drainage to stop Phytophthora root rot.
  2. Grow 2 rows of maize or sorghum around borders as barrier crops to intercept migrating Aphids & Whiteflies.
  3. Inspect curled leaves from both top and underside before choosing mitigation: Thrips curl leaves upward; Mites curl leaves downward.

Major Pests & Diseases

  • Upward Boat Curl (Thrips): Leaves curl upward and turn brittle; manage with blue sticky traps and timely irrigation.
  • Downward Inverted Curl (Yellow Mite): Leaves curl downward with elongated petioles ('rat-tail'); needs acaricide rotation not plain insecticides.
  • Fruit Rot / Anthracnose: Circular sunken black spots on red/mature chillies.
Safety Notice: Always verify pesticide registration, active ingredients, and exact per-hectare label dosages on official CIBRC (cibrc.gov.in) product labels or consult your nearest BCKV / UBKV / KVK agronomist before spraying. Never exceed recommended Waiting Periods (PHI).

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I tell chilli Thrips apart from Yellow Mites quickly?

Thrips make young leaves curl UPWARD like a boat; Yellow Mites make leaves curl DOWNWARD with long thin stalks.

Why plant tall maize rows around a chilli field?

Maize acts as a living windbreak barrier blocking incoming aphids and whiteflies carrying Chilli Leaf Curl Virus.

Why do waterlogged chilli plants suddenly wilt completely?

Chilli roots are highly sensitive to stagnant monsoon water and suffer Phytophthora collar rot on flat un-drained plots.
